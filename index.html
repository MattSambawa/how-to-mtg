Introduction to Magic: The Gathering

Explore lore rich worlds, discover unique strategies, and battle against your friends! Magic: The Gathering is a trading card game that can be played with two or more players.
With over 27,000 unique cards, there are infinite ways to play and various formats to choose from. One of the most popular formats is called Standard.


Basic Rules

  • Each player begins the game with a life total of 20. The goal is to reduce your opponent's life total to 0.
  • Standard Magic decks contain sixty cards. Each deck is composed of varying card types such as lands, creatures, and instances.
  • In order to cast spells, you need a resource called mana. Mana is produced by lands from your deck.
  • After casting creature spells, you place them on your side of the board to fight with your opponent! This part is known as the combat phase.
  • When combat resolves, the board enters the final phases, all creatures return to full health and the turn is passed to your opponent.

Lands

Lands are the primary mana source of a deck. You may play one land a turn on your main phases.

Click on the image

These are all various kinds of lands that have the same function as its respective color.


To obtain mana to cast spells, the player must indicate they are using a land by tapping the card.

The player indicates they are tapping a card by turning it 90 degrees to the side.


Creatures

Creatures are the bread and butter of a deck. In order to deal damage to an opponent, you need to construct a board of minions.


Instances and Sorceries

Instances and sorceries are ways to directly interact with you and your opponent's board state. Like creatures, these cards cost mana to play.
Instances are unique in the way that they can be used in between phases and during your opponent's turn.
